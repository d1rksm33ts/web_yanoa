About Dirk

Engineering curiosity into useful things.

Dirk Smeets is a Belgian engineer, software developer, teacher and creator behind YaNoa Engineering. He builds practical digital systems for connected homes, energy monitoring and telemetry, combining scientific curiosity with hands-on engineering.

Beyond software, Dirk explores the night sky through astrophotography as YaNoAstro and is active in amateur radio under the callsign ON1DGN. This site brings together his applications, personal projects and websites created or hosted for people, businesses and communities.
